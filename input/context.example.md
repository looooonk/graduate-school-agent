# Applicant Context

This file is injected into every pipeline stage (retrieval, judge, fit assessor)
to help focus research and evaluation on what matters most to you.

---

## Research Focus

I am primarily interested in **[your subfield, e.g. "natural language processing"
or "computational neuroscience"]**. When researching a program, prioritise
finding faculty working in this area and skip deep dives into unrelated subfields.

## Advisor Preferences

I am looking for advisors who:
- Work on [specific topic or methodology]
- Have active, well-funded labs (prefer NSF/NIH/DARPA-funded projects)
- Have graduated students in the last 3 years

## Application Profile Notes

- My GRE scores are [X verbal / Y quant] (or: I am not submitting GRE scores)
- My GPA is [X.X] from [institution type, e.g. "a large public R1"]
- My research experience is primarily in [area]
- I am a domestic / international applicant

## Constraints & Preferences

- I prefer programs in [geographic region or city, or "no preference"]
- I am open to / not open to fully-funded terminal Master's programs
- Funding is essential — I will only apply to programs with guaranteed stipends
- [Any other constraints, e.g. "I need a co-advisor in the CS department"]

## Notes for the Fit Assessor

When scoring fit, weight research alignment heavily (>50% of the score). A
program with two directly relevant advisors and mediocre applicant stats should
score higher than one with strong stats but no relevant faculty.
